# Reference: Stripe Design System

## What to steal from Stripe
- **Pricing page**: minimal grid, focus on the most popular plan, feature comparison table below.
- **Dashboard**: clean KPI cards, subtle chart styling, monospace for numbers.
- **Error states**: inline validation, human language, specific instructions.
- **Checkout**: single-column, no distractions, trust signals near the CTA.
- **Typography**: SF Pro / Inter, generous spacing, strong hierarchy.

## Key Patterns
- Gradient backgrounds for marketing pages (subtle, not garish).
- Border radius: 8px on cards, 4px on inputs.
- Shadows: `0 4px 6px rgba(0,0,0,0.07)` — barely visible, just depth.
- Colors: Very limited palette. Purple accent used sparingly.
