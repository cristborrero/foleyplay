# Reference: Framer Design Language

## What to steal from Framer
- **Marketing pages**: bold gradients, 3D elements, glass cards over dark backgrounds.
- **Component previews**: interactive, live demos embedded in docs.
- **Hero sections**: full-viewport, animated background, centered content.
- **Pricing**: 3 columns, middle highlighted, features checklist.
- **Glassmorphism**: `backdrop-blur-xl bg-white/10 border border-white/20` — canonical Framer look.

## Key Patterns
- Gradients: purple-to-pink, blue-to-cyan — very saturated.
- Dark backgrounds: `#0a0a0a` to `#111`.
- Typography: custom (Framer Sans). Substitute: Inter or Outfit.
- Radius: 16–24px on marketing cards. More rounded than product UI.
- Glow effects: `box-shadow: 0 0 80px rgba(120,80,255,0.3)`.

## Animation Patterns
- Scroll-triggered animations: fade-up, scale from 0.95 to 1.
- Parallax on hero background.
- Cursor follower glow effect.
- Number counters animating on scroll into view.

## What NOT to steal
- The extreme gradient overuse. Tone it down for FoleyPlay.
- 24px radius on everything. Use 8–12px for product UI.
