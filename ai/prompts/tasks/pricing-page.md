# Task: Pricing Page

## Goal
Convert free or trial users to paid subscribers.

## Layout
- Headline: "Elegí tu plan"
- Subheadline: "Cancelá cuando quieras. Sin compromisos."
- **Plan Cards**: 2–3 options, horizontal on desktop, vertical on mobile.
- **Toggle**: Monthly / Annual (with "Ahorrá 20%" badge on annual).
- **Feature comparison table**: below cards, collapsible on mobile.
- **FAQ**: 4–5 pricing-specific questions.
- **CTA Banner**: "¿Tenés preguntas? Contactanos."

## Plan Card Anatomy
- Plan name (H3)
- Price (Display size) + /mes or /año
- "Plan más popular" badge (on recommended)
- Feature list (checkmarks, 5–7 items)
- CTA button

## Design
- Recommended plan: slightly larger, `ring-2 ring-fp-lime`, subtle glow.
- Non-recommended: `border border-fp-border`.
- Price change on toggle: animated number transition.
- Annual: strikethrough monthly price, annual price + savings callout.
