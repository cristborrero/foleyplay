# Task: Landing Page

## Goal
Convert visitors into registered users or trial subscribers.

## Sections (in order)
1. **Hero**: Full-viewport, autoplay background video/image. Headline + subheadline + primary CTA + social proof count.
2. **Featured Content**: 3–5 highlighted titles with hover previews.
3. **Value Props**: 3 columns. Icon + title + 1 sentence each. Focus on what makes FoleyPlay unique.
4. **How it works**: 3 steps. Numbered, horizontal on desktop, vertical on mobile.
5. **Testimonials**: 2–3 quotes with avatar, name, role.
6. **Pricing Teaser**: Brief plan comparison. Primary CTA repeated.
7. **FAQ**: 5–7 questions. Accordion style.
8. **Footer CTA**: Full-width banner with headline + button.

## Design Directives
- Hero background: dark overlay on a cinematic backdrop image.
- Typography: Display-size headline (>48px). Max 8 words.
- CTA label: "Empezar gratis" or "Ver catálogo".
- Mobile: single column, hero height 100svh.
- Animations: stagger fade-in for hero content on load.

## Content Placeholders
- Headline: "Todo el cine. Sin límites."
- Subheadline: "Series, películas y más. Disponible cuando y donde quieras."
- CTA: "Empezar ahora →"
